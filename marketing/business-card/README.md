# Cloud2Tech Business Card

V2 flyer-inspired business card design.

## Files
- `cloud2tech-business-card-front.svg` — light V2-style front
- `cloud2tech-business-card-back.svg` — blue/cyan gradient back

## Design language
- Poppins typography
- Cobalt/royal blue, turquoise/cyan and warm yellow
- Soft, modern V2 flyer feel
- Clean whitespace and rounded geometry
- Professional small-business IT positioning

The contact details are currently placeholders and should be replaced before print if required.
