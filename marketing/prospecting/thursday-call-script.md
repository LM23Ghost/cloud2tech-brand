# Cloud2Tech — Thursday Prospecting Call Script

## 20-second opener
Hi, this is Lwazi from Cloud2Tech. We help small businesses with practical IT support — Microsoft 365, device setup and management, cybersecurity, cloud backup and general day-to-day IT. I’m calling to find out who currently looks after your IT and whether there are any recurring issues we could help simplify.

## Discovery questions
1. Who currently manages your email, Microsoft 365 and user accounts?
2. Do you have a regular process for onboarding staff, updates and backups?
3. What is the most frustrating IT issue your team deals with today?
4. Are you paying for ad-hoc support, or do you have a fixed monthly arrangement?
5. Would a short no-obligation IT check-up be useful?

## Positioning
- Professional support without the cost and complexity of a large internal IT team.
- Fixed, practical help for small businesses and startups.
- Cloud-first support across Microsoft 365, devices, security, backup and websites.
- Startup affordability pricing may be available for qualifying businesses.

## Close
Thanks — I’ll send a short overview with the areas we cover. If it looks relevant, we can book a 15-minute call to understand your setup and recommend the simplest next step.
