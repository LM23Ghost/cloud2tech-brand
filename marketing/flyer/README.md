# Cloud2Tech Business Flyer

One-page business flyer for digital distribution and print adaptation.

## Purpose

Introduce Cloud2Tech to small businesses and startups before prospecting calls.

## Brand direction

- Poppins typography
- Professional, clean and readable layout
- Blue / turquoise / yellow brand palette
- Tagline: **Technology that works. Business that grows.**
- No pricing claims in the flyer
- Contact details are placeholders until Cloud2Tech contact infrastructure is finalised

## File

- `cloud2tech-business-flyer.html` — A4-style responsive flyer suitable for browser viewing and print-to-PDF.
