# Cloud2Tech Logo Explorations

These concepts are experimental directions for the Cloud2Tech identity. The approved base logos in `logo/cloud2tech.svg` and `logo/cloud2tech-dark.svg` remain unchanged.

## Concept A — Integrated Wordmark
The cloud replaces the **o** in Cloud and the **T** in Tech becomes a subtle robot. The goal is to make the technology idea part of the name itself.

## Concept B — Digital Cloud
The cloud is constructed from connected digital paths and nodes. The robot treatment remains small and geometric. This direction leans into cloud, connectivity and modern IT.

## Concept C — Mascot
A separate cloud/robot character gives Cloud2Tech a recognisable personality. This can become especially useful for the education and Digital Classroom Initiative side of the brand while still supporting the main business identity.

## Concept D — Corporate
A more restrained, enterprise-friendly interpretation. The cloud and robot cues are integrated into the typography with minimal visual noise. Intended to feel established and scalable.

## Brand rules
- Primary typeface: Poppins
- Core colours: blue, turquoise/cyan, warm yellow accent
- Tagline is kept separate from the logo: **Technology that works. Business that grows.**
- Concepts should remain recognisable at small sizes and have a one-colour fallback for documents, invoices and print.

These are deliberately kept as separate files so the creative journey can be reviewed without overwriting the current logo assets.
